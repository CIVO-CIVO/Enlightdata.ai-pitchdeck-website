# Photo candidates

## POV slots

Slots: `pov-hero`, `pov-data`, `helmet-cam`, `everyday-pov`, `close`. Primaries are in `assets/src/<key>.jpg` and in `assets/photo-manifest.pov.json`. Alternates are in `assets/src/alt/`. Every licence below was re-checked on the source page: the Commons file page (licence template in the wikitext), plus the Flickr photo page for Flickr-origin files.

Search summary: Wikimedia Commons, through its API (full-text search, categories, `deepcat:` over the GoPro camera categories, and uploader galleries). Openverse was also used, but most multi-word queries returned HTTP 504 and the rest returned mostly irrelevant Flickr hits, so Flickr material was found mainly through Commons mirrors. Flickr licences and original sizes were read from the keyless photo and `/sizes/o/` pages. No true first-person photo of rail maintenance with an acceptable licence turned up. The only true-POV hand + tool + equipment frames found are by the Serbian photographer Nenad Stojković (Shixart1985, CC BY 2.0).

### Considered and rejected (for reference)

- **Rail track maintenance 20200801 013350** and **RailroadSwitch 20200801 014921** (Ka23 13, CC BY-SA 4.0): strong night views from above of track crews with cutting sparks, but taken at JR East in Japan, so outside Europe/North America.
- Other Prowelder87 / Weldscientist welding shots (Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0): "Shielded Metal Arc Welding .jpg" (outdoors, sparks, stormy sky) and "Modern Mig Welding.jpg" (welding under a truck; the truck shows a readable Washington licence plate). Good mood, not POV.
- Shixart1985 near-POV frames: a screwdriver on a heating thermostat, a brake-fluid cap over an engine, a nut on a grass-trimmer shaft. Domestic, not skilled field work.
- Raimond Spekking ICE depot series (Köln RESET 2016, Köln-Nippes 2018) and the Kecko Swiss track series: no close hands-at-work frames besides the one chosen.
- MTA: "Metro-North signal changes (11277044423)" (over-the-shoulder at a relay rack, but the face in profile dominates) and "Removal of third rail … Harold Interlocking (43378302354)" (night sparks, small figure).
- NASA spacewalk helmet-camera stills (public domain): real gloved-hands POV, but space, not one of the target fields.
